FAQ

Sawaal jo sabke mann mein hain.

Mera business expenses properly track nahi hote — kya ArvoLens help karega? +

Bilkul. ArvoLens specifically freelancers, shop owners, consultants aur small businesses ke liye bana hai. Har payment automatically detect hoti hai — UPI, card, cash — aur monthly business summary ready milti hai. CA ko data dena ab 1 tap ka kaam hai.

Kya app offline kaam karta hai? +

Manual entry, expense history dekhna, aur pehle se saved reports — ye sab offline kaam karte hain. AI features jaise receipt scan aur voice input ke liye internet connection chahiye kyunki ye AI-powered hain. Payment auto-detection bhi notification ke liye phone connected hona chahiye.

Sirf UPI track hoga ya baaki payments bhi? +

Sab kuch track hota hai — UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, credit card, debit card, bank transfer — koi bhi payment jiske liye bank notification aati hai, ArvoLens automatically detect karega. Cash ke liye voice ya manual entry available hai. Ek app — har payment method covered.

CA-ready report mein exactly kya hota hai? +

CA-ready report mein category-wise expense breakdown, payment method summary (UPI vs cash vs card), GST amounts (jahan applicable ho), merchant names, dates — sab organized format mein hota hai. PDF ya Excel mein export karo aur directly CA ko WhatsApp pe bhejo. Tax season preparation ka time 80% kam ho jaata hai.

Kya SMS permissions leni padti hain? +

Nahi. ArvoLens SMS access nahi karta — sirf notification access use karta hai. Ye Play Store guidelines ke according hai aur tumhara data zyada safe rehta hai. Bank notifications se automatically transactions detect hoti hain — bina kisi SMS permission ke.

Android aur iOS dono pe available hai? +

Haan — dono pe available hoga. Android pe sabhi features fully kaam karte hain including auto payment detection. iOS pe scan, voice input, manual entry — sab perfectly kaam karta hai. Auto payment detection iOS pe Apple ke system restrictions ki wajah se limited ho sakta hai — Android pe best experience milta hai.

Mera financial data safe hai? +

Bilkul. Data local device pe encrypted store hota hai. Biometric lock se sirf tum access kar sakte ho. Data kabhi third party ko nahi becha jaata. Cloud backup optional hai — by default sab kuch aapke phone mein. Aapka paisa, aapki privacy — 100%.
